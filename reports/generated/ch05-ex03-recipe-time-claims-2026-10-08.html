Checking Recipe Time Claims

A small rule-based evaluator can separate clear time-limit failures from uncertain recipe timing that needs human review.

October 8, 2026 · Chapter 5: Implementing Automated Evaluators · Recipe Bot time evaluator

1 · The question

Can a deterministic evaluator classify whether a recipe fits the user's stated time limit without hiding uncertainty?

Clear Fail

SYN069 asks for a dish in 30–60 minutes. Required lentils soak for at least 60 minutes, then pressure cook for at least 15 minutes and simmer for at least 15 minutes.

Count what overlaps

The masala cooks while the lentils pressure cook. Those minutes overlap, so the known minimum is 60 + 15 + 15 = 90 minutes, not the sum of every listed step.

In plain terms: Even before counting the unstated natural pressure release, the recipe needs at least 90 minutes. That already exceeds the 60-minute cap.

2 · The decision rule

LabelUse it whenExample
PassA supported total fits within the requested cap.Synthetic fixture: 32 minutes against a 30-minute target with 5-minute tolerance.
FailA complete estimate or known minimum exceeds the cap.SYN069: known minimum 90 minutes against a 60-minute cap.
ReviewMissing timing details could change the result, or the request is vague.SYN017: cooking time is stated, but several required prep times are not.
Rule: Missing details call for Review only when they leave the result uncertain. A known minimum that already exceeds the cap proves Fail.

3 · Reproduce the lab

From the repository root, this command applies the rules to structured case annotations. It does not read the recipe text or estimate durations.

Command and observed result

uv run --no-project python projects/recipe-bot-time-evaluator/check.py

Before SYN069 was added, the owner observed 8/8 label matches. After adding SYN069, the checker was updated to handle known minimum times; the updated version still needs a run.

Limitation: The human supplies the time limit and timing annotations. A matching label count checks that code follows those annotations; it does not prove the annotations are correct. The homework traces are synthetic course examples, not production data.

4 · Next steps

  • Run the updated checker and confirm SYN069 is Fail while uncertain cases stay Review.
  • Review another timed trace with overlapping steps and decide whether its timing supports Pass, Fail, or Review.
Takeaway: A known lower bound can prove a time claim fails even when the full recipe duration is unknown.
