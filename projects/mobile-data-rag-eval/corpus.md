
# Synthetic mobile-support FAQ

## DOC-A — Bundle expiry
The 5 GB monthly plan expires 30 days after activation. Unused data does not carry over.

## DOC-B — Connection troubleshooting
If mobile data is on but apps cannot connect, toggle airplane mode off and on. If that does not help, restart the phone.

## DOC-C — Check remaining data
To see how much data remains, open the account app and select Usage.

## DOC-D — Billing dates
The next billing date is the first day of each month. Billing-cycle changes are listed in Plan Details in the account app.
