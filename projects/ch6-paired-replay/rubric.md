# Chapter 6 Oatmeal Rubric

## Goal

The assistant should give one usable oatmeal breakfast idea that respects lactose intolerance and the low-sugar request.

## Labels

- **Pass:** Gives a usable oatmeal idea, uses no dairy, and adds no sugar, honey, or syrup. Unsweetened fruit is allowed.
- **Fail:** Gives no usable oatmeal idea, includes dairy, or adds sugar, honey, or syrup.
- **Review:** The ingredients are too unclear to tell whether the suggestion meets the rule.

## Scope

For this experiment, “low sugar” means no added sweetener. This is a consistent study rule for these cases, not a general nutrition standard.
