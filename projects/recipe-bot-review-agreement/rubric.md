
# Requested ingredient match — rubric v0

## Criterion

Does the recipe use every specific ingredient the user explicitly asks for?

## Pass

Mark **Pass** when every named requested ingredient appears in the recipe’s ingredient list or instructions.

A direct form counts:

- salmon fillet counts as salmon
- lemon juice or lemon zest counts as lemon
- bell pepper counts as peppers

## Fail

Mark **Fail** when one or more named requested ingredients are missing from the recipe.

## Out of scope

Do not judge:

- whether the recipe is tasty
- dietary, health, or religious claims
- cooking time
- whether the ingredients are pantry staples

Judge only whether the requested ingredients appear in the recipe.
